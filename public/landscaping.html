A garden is a four-season job

Here's what we're actually doing at your place through the year.

  • Spring

    Wake it up

    Beds cleared and mulched, first cut, hard pruning done before the growth starts.

  • Summer

    Keep it sharp

    Regular mowing, edging, deadheading and watering through the dry weeks.

  • Autumn

    Plant and clear

    The best planting window of the year, plus leaves, bulbs and lawn repair.

  • Winter

    Build and prune

    Structural work, tree pruning and hardscaping while the beds are dormant.

How it goes

Four steps, and you can stop after any of them.

  1. We walk the garden together

    Free, about an hour, and you'll get honest advice even if you never hire us.

  2. A fixed quote within a week

    Itemised, with a plan drawing if you're doing a design. No hourly surprises.

  3. We build it

    You'll know which days we're on site and who's coming. We clear up every evening.

  4. We look after it

    Optional, but most people stay on. A garden left alone for a year undoes a lot of work.
