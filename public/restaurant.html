The kitchen

One grill, one small room.

Vela opened in a former ship chandler's on Front Street in 2019 with twenty-eight seats and no gas line. Everything that gets heat gets it from oak and hickory.

We buy from four boats out of Masonboro and Wrightsville and two farms up the Cape Fear. What they land on Monday is what you eat on Friday, which is why the menu is short and why it moves.

There's no dress code, no tasting menu and no minimum spend. Come and eat two things at the bar if that's the evening you want.
